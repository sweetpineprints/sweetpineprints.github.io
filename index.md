# Privacy Policy — Sweet Pine Prints (sweetpine-pinner)

Effective: July 25, 2026

sweetpine-pinner is an internal tool used solely by Sweet Pine Prints to
schedule and publish pins of our own products to our own Pinterest boards,
and to read performance statistics for those pins.

- We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from Pinterest
  users. The app accesses only our own account's boards, pins, and
  analytics via the Pinterest API.
- No third parties receive any data. No advertising, tracking, or
  profiling of any kind is performed.
- API credentials are stored securely on our own private systems and are
  never shared.
- For any questions: contact us via our Etsy shop,
  https://sweetpineprints.etsy.com

We comply with the Pinterest Developer Guidelines and applicable data
protection laws.
